# Season plan — levels first

Written 2026-11-27 (season day 35) by the planning chat with Alex. **The goal (Alex, 11-27): level the characters the
way the board's fleets did; achievements are taken on the way, where the level already allows them.** Chasing
achievements head-on costs more than walking through them with levelled characters.

Alex does not re-check the reasoning, so **every line carries its reference**: a count over the board's data and
named characters (`account/character`) that can be looked up. Where the board does not show something, the line
says so.

Sources:
- **S1** — the board read of 2026-11-27 16:00 UTC (`logs/board/2026-11-27/1600Z`): 40 accounts (everyone above us
  and our neighbours), 200 characters, the ladder.
- **S2** — `docs/strategy/board/whole-1.md` (task 026b): 15 reads of 11-24/25, 1,242 character-intervals with
  combat XP. B.2 = what is fought at each level, C = what the fastest quarter of a band wears, A = how crafts are
  levelled.
- **S3** — `docs/strategy/024/base/` (30 accounts read on 11-22/23): achievement dates; the same characters
  against S1 give 4–5 days of progress.

## 6. What we stop doing

- No craft is trained past what the stage asks (the standing targets jewelrycrafting 35 and gearcrafting 35 wait for
  stage 3).
