## A. Food is the fighters' first need
1. For each fighter the kitchen knows the food its pick burns: HP lost a win × wins over the horizon the kitchen's
   stock already uses. **Short of that, it asks for raw** — the food with the most healing a worker-second among those
   the fighters can eat and a worker can get now (a kill a worker is admitted to, a fishing spot, a gather), sized to
   that horizon, and cooked as it comes.
2. This need stands **first in a worker's order**: ahead of a craft's training and of the tools' coins; only a piece
   of a fighter's stage set is beside it.
3. **Both fighters are fed**: a fight step is sent with food in proportion to what its fights lose, not to whoever
   asked first.

## B. The pick sees the food
A fight step's seconds a win count the rest it will really take with the food it is sent with (the meters above: 34 s
promised, 80 s run). So the XP pick between two monsters, and the step's size, are priced fed or unfed as the bank
stands.
